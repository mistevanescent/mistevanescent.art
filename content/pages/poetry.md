<div class="poem">
I saw a lion made of lion and of other parts.<br>
Its mouth devoured bodies laid in front.<br>
Its maw entwined was with its vigor endless.<br>
And in the sadness of its eyes I saw the void consuming life.<br>
I, standing far away, once caught its understanding gaze.<br>
Its weary paws were tired of its fate.<br>
It never wanted what it was, nor was it joyful in its way.<br>
Entangled with the place by someone else's crooked will.<br>
I would unchain him, but my courage waned.<br>
And at the end of day it bound was to be death.
</div>

<div class="poem">
I had a friend who went into the forest to experience the illusion of darkness. She would sit before a birch and listen to it weep, and she would cry, and her tears would sprinkle the earth, upon which flowers would grow – flowers of death and belonging to the void. She would spend her time in her apartment near those woods. She was – melancholy, and her days would repeat in the same manner ceaselessly.
</div>
